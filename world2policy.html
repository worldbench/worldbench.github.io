Policy–Environment Co-Evolution

Abstract

Learning to drive in worlds that evolve with the policy.

Interactive Gaussian worlds enable photorealistic closed-loop simulation for autonomous driving. However, sustained policy improvement requires addressing two coupled challenges: how to resolve heterogeneous safety constraints during policy learning, and which scenarios should support continual policy updates. A monolithic policy often entangles avoidance behaviors across dynamic actors, static obstacles, and road boundaries, while fixed scenario distributions yield diminishing returns as the policy improves. We present World2Policy, a co-evolutionary framework unifying policy optimization with failure-conditioned scenario generation. In the Policy Optimization Engine, three complementary constraint-specialized experts decouple safety modeling across foreground dynamics, static hazards, and drivable-area constraints via constrained reinforcement learning, while a risk-aware selector evaluates trajectory-specific risks and balances them with progress and comfort to choose the final trajectory. In the Environment Engine, closed-loop rollouts expose residual failures, guiding the synthesis of physically plausible, recoverable scenarios near the policy's capability boundary and updating the training curriculum for policy optimization. Experiments on the closed-loop HUGSIM benchmark show that integrating World2Policy with UniAD, VAD, and LTF achieves HD-Score gains of +4.9, +6.1, and +6.9 points, respectively. Crucially, the co-evolutionary loop supports continual policy improvement, yielding curriculum-level gains across successive updates.

A Failure-Driven Learning Loop

Method

Two complementary engines.
One continually improving driving policy.

Policy Optimization Engine

Complementary experts. Safer decisions.

A frozen base planner supplies BEV features and a nominal trajectory. Three experts learn complementary refinements with constrained PPO, sharing a driving objective while emphasizing different safety risks.

Dynamic actors

Foreground expert

Emphasizes collision risks from moving actors, refining interactions with surrounding traffic.

Static obstacles

Background expert

Emphasizes collision risks from static hazards, supporting obstacle-aware trajectory refinements.

Drivable area

Drivable expert

Emphasizes drivable-area violation risks, keeping candidate motions within valid road regions.

Constrained learning

A shared goal, specialized safety budgets

Every expert considers all three risks. Expert-specific cost weights emphasize its designated constraint, while the common reward encourages progress, task completion, and comfort. A Lagrange multiplier adapts to safety-budget violations during CPPO training.

Risk-aware selection

Choose the trajectory, balance the trade-offs

Each expert samples a driving mode and residual refinements of the base trajectory. The selector uses BEV history, ego state, and each candidate trajectory to predict dynamic collision, static collision, and off-road risks, then balances these risks with analytical progress and comfort.

Expert proposalsRisk-aware selectorFinal trajectory

The selector is trained offline on candidate-specific simulator rollouts, then frozen.

Environment Engine

Turn today's failures into tomorrow's curriculum.

Closed-loop evaluation exposes the current policy's weaknesses. Targeted scenario generation transforms these failures into physically plausible, recoverable challenges near its capability boundary.

Mine residual failures

Roll out the current policy and extract interaction windows preceding collisions, off-road events, or stagnation.

Generate targeted scenarios

Use cross-entropy search to perturb critical actors' poses and motion parameters while preserving the reconstructed background.

Keep valid, recoverable cases

Filter for physical validity and verify a safe, comfortable recovery with nontrivial progress using a fixed action lattice.

Two-Engine Collaboration

Each round adds accepted scenarios to the training pool and updates the experts with the base planner and selector frozen. The improved policy reveals new residual failures, guiding the next round.
